# 2026 Annual Planning Meeting

This meeting is open to the public!

Together, let's make this year count.

Big ideas start with great conversations! Join us for our Annual Planning Meeting as we brainstorm programs, events, and opportunities to strengthen our community and empower voters in the year ahead.

Bring your favorite salad, finger food, or dessert to share, and come ready to connect, collaborate, and help shape the year ahead.
Whether you're a longtime member or just curious about getting involved, your ideas matter. We hope to see you there!

When: Thursday, August 6, 2026, 5:00 PM

Where: Kat Odell's House in Pullman

**Let’s plan, laugh, and eat together, and turn ideas into action.**
