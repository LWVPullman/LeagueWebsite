# Girl Scout Promote the Vote Patch Work

Organizer: Fran Powers, Youth Civics Connection Committee Chair

When: Sunday, October, 18, 1:30-3:30 pm

Where: Simpson Methodist Church, Pullman

Girl Scouts from throughout Whitman County gathered for a day of activities designed to help them earn their “Promote the Vote” patch while learning about civics and the importance of participating in our democracy.

The event kicked off with a lively civics game, followed by three interactive activity stations focused on encouraging voter participation. Scouts created voting-themed postcards, explored a timeline of voting history, and learned the steps involved in voting—from registering to vote to completing and returning a ballot.

The girls also had an opportunity to hear from Pullman City Council Member Ann Parks and former Council Member Calla DeLira, who shared their experiences as elected officials and answered questions about serving and representing our community.

It was a fun, hands-on learning experience that introduced the next generation of voters to the importance of making their voices heard!
